# LifeMine Therapeutics

LifeMine Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company headquartered in Watertown, Massachusetts, with sites in Gloucester, Massachusetts and Basel, Switzerland. It pioneers a Top-Down Drug Discovery approach that leverages evolutionary insights from fungi to find next-generation precision medicines.

Its LifeBank collection of deep-sequenced wild-type fungi and its Avatar-Rx platform combine human genetics and genomics, bioinformatics, machine learning and synthetic biology to mine biosynthetic gene clusters for genetically encoded small molecules, using embedded target genes as avatars to predict therapeutic effect independent of chemical structure. Lead program LIFE-001 is a precision-engineered calcineurin inhibitor in Phase 1 for immune-mediated disorders and organ transplant rejection, alongside a genomic drug discovery alliance with GSK.
