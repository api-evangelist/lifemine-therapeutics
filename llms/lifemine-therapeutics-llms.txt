# LifeMine Therapeutics

> LifeMine Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in Watertown, Massachusetts applying genomics, bioinformatics, machine learning and synthetic biology to fungal biology. Its Top-Down Drug Discovery approach searches a genomicized fungal strain collection for genetically encoded small molecules whose biosynthetic gene clusters carry embedded target genes, using function rather than chemical structure as the starting point for precision medicines.

## Platform

- LifeBank: a fully genomicized fungal strain collection of deep-sequenced wild-type fungi spanning tens of thousands of species, assembled in part from legacy pharmaceutical collections.
- Avatar-Rx: the computational platform that analyzes fungal DNA for biosynthetic gene clusters and identifies embedded target genes (ETaGs) that act as avatars for predicting therapeutic effect independent of chemical structure.
- Genetically Encoded Small Molecules (GEMs): the compound class the platform surfaces as drug leads.
